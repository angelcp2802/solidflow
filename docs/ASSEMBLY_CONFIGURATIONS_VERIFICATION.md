# Independent configured geometry verification

The standalone [selected-state solve transaction](ASSEMBLY_CONFIGURATIONS_SOLVE.md)
uses this final gate after complete child-before-parent staging. The verifier
itself remains a detached measurement API without edit authority.

This standalone layer measures the complete current candidate configuration.
It follows the [numerical scopes and state plan](ASSEMBLY_CONFIGURATIONS_SCOPE.md)
and [native relation inventory](ASSEMBLY_CONFIGURATIONS_RELATIONS.md). It does
not execute that plan, change source/catalog declarations, preserve a movement
trajectory, write captures, or authorize a saved edit or Apply operation.

## Physical measurements

The new binary measurement dispatch covers the nineteen current binary mate
types. It composes authenticated native supports in their actual effective
placements, then uses physical vector, quaternion, distance, radius and phase
formulas. It does not call numerical residual rows, Jacobians or the solver as
an independent oracle. Pure parameter/conversion definitions and the neutral
twist coordinate are shared mathematical definitions, not native grants.

Plane distances retain their oriented signed coordinate. Direction mates keep
their plane/axis semantics. Concentric and screw checks qualify actual axes;
rotation locks and component locks compare proper relative rotations. Principal
and directed angular intervals retain their declared bounds, activity and
coordinate conventions, including the directed complement cut. Tangencies use
genuine surface purposes, current radii, declared branches/sides and native
material senses. Normal-relation metadata describes infinite analytic supports;
it does not establish finite face contact, body containment or noninterference.

Gear, screw and rack checks compare the actual neutral-relative twist lifted
near the authored angle. Active checks also measure their physical equation.
Suppressed mechanical declarations check that stored angular coordinate only;
they do not enforce the suppressed equation or consume irrelevant positional
uncertainty. No historical turns are inferred independently of that authored
anchor, and this layer cannot certify a path between two contexts.

Centered Width independently compares the two ordered sum/difference plane
bisectors, their oriented normals and offsets. A branch whose normal cannot be
resolved returns `precisionLimit`; it is never replaced with another branch.
This does not implement Free, Dimension or Percent Width modes. Symmetry
compares the physical reflection of point, plane, LINE/cylinder axis, full
cylinder surface or full sphere surface with its declared target and mirror.
An axis-only or direction-only grant cannot substitute for a material surface.

## Precision and native identity

The new measurements consume strict positive requested linear/angular bands,
defaulting to 1e-6 mm and 1e-8 radians. Angular bands must be below pi/2. They
never enlarge a requested band to match native topology tolerance. Linear
uncertainty retains original effective-chain depth and translation magnitudes,
even when large parent/child translations cancel. Points compose directly
around a nearby anchor; no inverse world pose recovers a lost small offset.
Direction-only comparisons omit positional error and do not interpret native
topological length tolerance as angular metrology.

For the new binary, Width and Symmetry measurements, retained error must fit
the requested band. A whole residual envelope inside is satisfied; a whole
envelope outside at least one condition is unsatisfied. An overlap, unresolved
chart/branch, or insufficient finite error budget is `precisionLimit`. Reports
contain finite values or explicit nulls. These are conservative Float64 error
estimates, not a general interval-arithmetic or physical metrology certificate.
Existing hinge and advanced profile/path measurements keep their documented
family-specific acceptance and uncertainty policies.

Measurement functions borrow actual configured aggregates. Entry and exit
checks authenticate the native session, complete live context and privately
issued immutable role identities, including precision-limit returns. Copies,
legacy proofs, another session's observations and stale generations reject.
Tuple, selected configuration, ancestry, declaring scope and full component
paths remain explicit. Equal intrinsic states in different placed instances
retain different physical grants. These detached reports confer no proof or
edit authority. Controllers must advance generation on context changes,
including switching away and back.

## Fresh candidate gate

`verifyConfiguredAssemblyGeometry(input, options)` owns a new native snapshot
for the complete document, catalog, root selector and generation. It resolves
every active declaration at every actual placed scope, plus suppressed
gear/screw/rack continuity records. It uses the nineteen binary checks, centered
Width, five Symmetry variants and the existing independent profile, LINE,
circular path and four/six-role hinge measurements. It does not deduplicate
placed uses according to the intrinsic solve plan.

Keys contain the full scope path, definition id/revision, selected configuration
and mate id. Reports retain the actual role paths and context metadata. All
reports are provisional until final reauthentication of every aggregate and
the complete context, and successful disposal of the owned snapshot. Captured
native implementation methods prevent a later public inventory override from
turning a missing inventory into an empty successful check. Existing advanced
fixed-arc observations and hinge authentication also use captured native methods.

An unavailable active reference rejects native resolution. Unavailable passive
references remain explicit diagnostics and make the result unaccepted; winding
is not discarded. `verified`, `unsatisfied`, `precisionLimit` and
`references-unavailable` distinguish complete results. A fatal native query,
context change or release error throws and withholds the whole report. A
successful empty check means only that the selected context has no active or
available passive relations to check.

## Remaining transaction and product work

A configured solve still needs child-before-parent execution, exact-state
staging, fresh geometry gating of the staged output, winding/capture repair and
atomic base/named-state writeback. The verifier alone does not supply them.
Parameter/part-feature variants, flexible instances, persistence/versioning,
editor selection, preview/Apply, history/recovery and dependent consumers remain
required. This prerequisite does not close the complete assembly/demo gate or
establish platform support. Actual tests and their limits are recorded in
[VALIDATION.md](VALIDATION.md).
