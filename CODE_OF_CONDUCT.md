# Code of Conduct

SolidFlow welcomes people with different backgrounds, languages and levels of CAD or programming experience. Help others participate and keep disagreements focused on the work.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and specific. Explain a problem with evidence and suggest a practical next step.
- Assume questions and mistakes are opportunities to clarify. Accept corrections and give credit for others' work.
- Respect people's time, privacy and boundaries. Ask before sharing private conversations or personal information.
- Discuss technical decisions without attacking the person proposing them.

Harassment, discriminatory insults, threats, sexual harassment, stalking, doxxing and deliberate disruption are not acceptable. Retaliation against someone who reports a concern is also unacceptable.

This policy applies to project issues, pull requests, discussions and other project spaces, and when someone explicitly represents SolidFlow elsewhere.

## Reporting a concern

Use GitHub's **Report content** option on the relevant comment, issue or pull request. Where available, choose reporting to repository administrators. For platform abuse, threats or a concern involving an administrator, use [GitHub's abuse-reporting process](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).

If you need a private project contact and no suitable reporting option is available, open an issue asking an administrator to arrange one. Do not include names, allegations, private screenshots or other sensitive details in that public request. Share evidence only once an appropriate private channel is established. This project does not publish a dedicated conduct email address or promise a confidential channel that has not been arranged.

Report software vulnerabilities through the separate [security policy](SECURITY.md).

## How maintainers respond

Repository administrators handle reports on a best-effort basis, limit access to sensitive information and avoid unnecessary public disclosure. They may clarify expectations, request a correction, remove content, restrict participation or ban an account, according to the seriousness and repetition of the behavior. An explanation should be given to the affected person where practical.

Someone involved in a report should not be its sole reviewer when another administrator is available. A request to reconsider a moderation decision can be made through the same reporting route; another administrator should review it where one is available. This policy does not imply a staffed response service or a guaranteed response time.
